Wizard art preview

Puts wizards together from the sheets in public/art/wizards/ and data/wizard-art.json, and recolours them the way the game will: each robe and hat sheet is recoloured once on a canvas by swapping the key greys for the colour's ramp, then the layers are drawn in layerOrder at scale with nearest-neighbour scaling. Top: your wizard facing right and mirrored (as on the right of the circle). Below: the same outfit in all 8 colours, and every hat on every robe, all playing the chosen animation. Open from the dev server: npm run dev, then /pass-the-spark/tools/art/preview.html.
